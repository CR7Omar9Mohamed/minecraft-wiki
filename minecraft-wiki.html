Overview

A creeper is a hostile mob that silently stalks players and detonates when it gets close. With its green, mottled body and four stubby legs, it is one of the most recognizable creatures in Minecraft.

Behavior

Creepers approach players within about sixteen blocks. Once close enough, the fuse starts with a hiss and lasts 1.5 seconds. Back away and the fuse stops. After a lightning strike a creeper becomes charged, and its blast is twice as strong. Creepers keep away from cats and ocelots.

Spawning

Creepers spawn in dark areas of the Overworld, on solid blocks, in caves, forests and at night on the surface. Lighting up your base with torches keeps them from appearing nearby.

Trivia

  • The creeper was born from a bug, when its creator mixed up height and width values while making a pig model.
  • Its explosion damages terrain but cannot break bedrock.
  • It has no arms, so it can only hurt you by going boom.
